# <Brand /> answers your customers and books their appointments, in Arabic and English

<Brand /> replies from your own services, prices and policies, shows where each answer came from,
and books, moves or cancels appointments. No booking is made until the customer confirms it.

This website is still being built. The product is already live: try the demo, or read the code.
